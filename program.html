Program

52 weeks, 9 phases. It starts with the language of film and moves through classical theory, auteur and genre, semiotics and psychoanalysis, cognitive theory, documentary, digital cinema and the cinema of Turkey. Each phase builds on the one before. Weeks with published lessons are clickable.

Phase I, Week 1–5

Foundations

Language before theory: you can’t discuss theory until you can name a shot, a cut, a sound. This phase builds the vocabulary of analysis.

  • Week 1What is form?PublishedCitizen Kane (Welles, 1941)
  • Week 2NarrativePublishedRashomon (Kurosawa, 1950); optional Memento (Nolan, 2000)
  • Week 3Mise-en-scène and cinematographyPublishedTokyo Story (Ozu, 1953) + Touch of Evil (Welles, 1958) opening long take
  • Week 4Editing and soundPublishedÀ bout de souffle (Godard, 1960) + The Conversation (Coppola, 1974)
  • Week 5What is film theory? Draw the mapPublishedSherlock Jr. (Keaton, 1924)

Phase II, Week 6–12

Classical theory

The central quarrel of classical theory: is what makes cinema an art its transformation of reality (the formalists, montage) or its recording of reality (the realists)? By the end of this phase you should be able to argue both sides.

  • Week 6Is cinema an art? Münsterberg and ArnheimPublishedSunrise (Murnau, 1927); Das Cabinet des Dr. Caligari (Wiene, 1920)
  • Week 7The face, photogénie and the cinema of attractionsLumière short films (1895–97); Le Voyage dans la Lune (Méliès, 1902); La Passion de Jeanne d'Arc (Dreyer, 1928)
  • Week 8Soviet montage I: Kuleshov, Pudovkin, VertovChelovek s kino-apparatom / Man with a Movie Camera (Vertov, 1929); Mat (Pudovkin, 1926)
  • Week 9Soviet montage II: EisensteinBronenosets Potyomkin (1925) Odessa Steps; Oktyabr (1928) “gods” sequence; modern example: The Godfather (1972) baptism sequence
  • Week 10Bazin I: ontology and deep focusLa Règle du jeu (Renoir, 1939); The Best Years of Our Lives (Wyler, 1946)
  • Week 11Bazin II and Italian NeorealismLadri di biciclette (De Sica, 1948); Roma città aperta (Rossellini, 1945); Umberto D. (De Sica, 1952); for connection, Uzak (Ceylan, 2002) and Close-Up (Kiarostami, 1990)
  • Week 12KracauerM (Lang, 1931); Nanook of the North (Flaherty, 1922)

Phase III, Week 13–18

Auteur, genre, star

Where does a film’s meaning come from: the director, the genre, the star, the studio system? This phase tests all three answers.

  • Week 13Auteur I: France and Cahiers du cinémaLes Quatre Cents Coups (Truffaut, 1959); Rear Window (Hitchcock, 1954); Journal d'un curé de campagne (Bresson, 1951)
  • Week 14Auteur II: America and the structuralist auteurThe Searchers (Ford, 1956); Rio Bravo (Hawks, 1959); Bringing Up Baby (Hawks, 1938)
  • Week 15The death of the author, and the auteur as brandThe Grand Budapest Hotel (Anderson, 2014); 3 films by a director you love, over the course of this month
  • Week 16Genre I: the gangster film and the westernScarface (Hawks, 1932); Stagecoach (Ford, 1939); Unforgiven (Eastwood, 1992)
  • Week 17Genre II: theory, film noir, melodramaDouble Indemnity (Wilder, 1944); All That Heaven Allows (Sirk, 1955); optional Chinatown (Polanski, 1974), Get Out (Peele, 2017)
  • Week 18Classical Hollywood and starsCasablanca (Curtiz, 1942); Gentlemen Prefer Blondes (Hawks, 1953)

Phase IV, Week 19–29

Semiotics, ideology, psychoanalysis, feminism

The “grand theory” era of the 1960s–80s: can film be decoded like a language, how does it position the spectator, whose gaze does it distribute? The hardest and most important phase of the program; the texts are dense, so don’t rush.

  • Week 19Semiotics I: Saussure and BarthesJulius Caesar (Mankiewicz, 1953); Ivan Grozny (Eisenstein, 1944)
  • Week 20Semiotics II: Metz and WollenAdieu Philippine (Rozier, 1962), which Metz breaks down into syntagmas; Psycho (Hitchcock, 1960)
  • Week 21Structuralist narrative and textual analysisThe Birds (Hitchcock, 1963); Star Wars (Lucas, 1977)
  • Week 22The Frankfurt School and BenjaminModern Times (Chaplin, 1936); Triumph des Willens (Riefenstahl, 1935), as an analysis of propaganda
  • Week 23Althusser and the Cahiers critique of ideologyYoung Mr. Lincoln (Ford, 1939)
  • Week 24Brecht, Godard and counter-cinemaWeek-end (Godard, 1967); Tout va bien (Godard & Gorin, 1972); 2 ou 3 choses que je sais d'elle (Godard, 1967)
  • Week 25Third CinemaLa hora de los hornos (1968), part one; Deus e o Diabo na Terra do Sol (Rocha, 1964); Memorias del subdesarrollo (Gutiérrez Alea, 1968); La battaglia di Algeri (Pontecorvo, 1966); for connection, Umut (Güney, 1970)
  • Week 26Foundations of psychoanalysisVertigo (Hitchcock, 1958); Persona (Bergman, 1966)
  • Week 27Apparatus theory and suturePeeping Tom (Powell, 1960); The Pervert's Guide to Cinema (Fiennes & Žižek, 2006)
  • Week 28Feminist film theory IVertigo (again); Morocco (von Sternberg, 1930); Jeanne Dielman, 23 quai du Commerce, 1080 Bruxelles (Akerman, 1975)
  • Week 29Feminist film theory IIHalloween (Carpenter, 1978); Stella Dallas (Vidor, 1937); Cléo de 5 à 7 (Varda, 1962); Portrait de la jeune fille en feu (Sciamma, 2019)

Phase V, Week 30–36

Cognitive theory, Deleuze, phenomenology

After the 1980s, a break with grand theory in three different directions: approaching the spectator as a mind (cognitive theory), cinema as a form of thought (Deleuze), and film as bodily experience (phenomenology).

  • Week 30Post-Theory and neoformalismBanshun / Late Spring (Ozu, 1949); Pickpocket (Bresson, 1959); Playtime (Tati, 1967)
  • Week 31Cognitive theory: spectator and characterMemento (again); Lola rennt (Tykwer, 1998); The Talented Mr. Ripley (Minghella, 1999)
  • Week 32Deleuze I: the movement-imageShichinin no samurai / Seven Samurai (Kurosawa, 1954); La Passion de Jeanne d'Arc (again)
  • Week 33Deleuze II: crisis and the time-imageGermania anno zero (Rossellini, 1948); L'avventura (Antonioni, 1960); L'Année dernière à Marienbad (Resnais, 1961)
  • Week 34Deleuze III: sheets of the past and thoughtZerkalo / Mirror (Tarkovsky, 1975); Bir Zamanlar Anadolu'da (Ceylan, 2011); Citizen Kane (again)
  • Week 35PhenomenologyThe Piano (Campion, 1993), the film with which Sobchack’s essay opens; Beau Travail (Denis, 1999)
  • Week 36Affect and haptic visualityIn the Mood for Love (Wong Kar-wai, 2000); Uncle Boonmee Who Can Recall His Past Lives (Weerasethakul, 2010); Leviathan (Castaing-Taylor & Paravel, 2012)

Phase VI, Week 37–43

Spectators, identity and documentary

Real spectators instead of theory’s “universal spectator”: how do different people read the same film, who is absent from the screen, and how does documentary construct truth?

  • Week 37Cultural studies and receptionThe Rocky Horror Picture Show (Sharman, 1975); The Wizard of Oz (Fleming, 1939)
  • Week 38Spectatorship, modernity and fan cultureIntolerance (Griffith, 1916); Star Wars (again, through the lens of fan culture)
  • Week 39Race, representation and the oppositional gazeThe Birth of a Nation (Griffith, 1915), viewed critically; Killer of Sheep (Burnett, 1978); Do the Right Thing (Lee, 1989); Daughters of the Dust (Dash, 1991)
  • Week 40Postcolonial theory and accented cinemaLa Noire de… (Sembène, 1966); Touki Bouki (Mambéty, 1973); Gegen die Wand / Head-On (Akın, 2004); Lawrence of Arabia (Lean, 1962), for a reading of Orientalism
  • Week 41Queer theoryRope (Hitchcock, 1948); Paris Is Burning (Livingston, 1990); Happy Together (Wong Kar-wai, 1997); Lola + Bilidikid (Ataman, 1999); Carol (Haynes, 2015)
  • Week 42Documentary I: modesNuit et brouillard (Resnais, 1956); Chronique d'un été (Rouch & Morin, 1961); Primary (Drew, 1960); Salesman (Maysles brothers, 1969)
  • Week 43Documentary II: truth, memory, reenactmentThe Thin Blue Line (Morris, 1988); Sans Soleil (Marker, 1983); Reassemblage (Trinh, 1982); The Act of Killing (Oppenheimer, 2012); Stories We Tell (Polley, 2012); optional: one part of Shoah (Lanzmann, 1985)

Phase VII, Week 44–46

Postmodernism and digital cinema

Films are now mostly not shot on film and not watched in theaters. This phase returns to the Bazinian question from the start of the book: what is cinema once the image’s bond with reality is broken?

  • Week 44PostmodernismBody Heat (Kasdan, 1981); Blue Velvet (Lynch, 1986); Blade Runner (Scott, 1982); Pulp Fiction (Tarantino, 1994); The Truman Show (Weir, 1998)
  • Week 45Digital I: the crisis of the indexJurassic Park (Spielberg, 1993); The Matrix (Wachowskis, 1999); Russkiy kovcheg / Russian Ark (Sokurov, 2002); Gravity (Cuarón, 2013)
  • Week 46Cinema after cinemaDemonlover (Assayas, 2002); Boarding Gate (Assayas, 2007); Tangerine (Baker, 2015, shot on an iPhone); Searching (Chaganty, 2018); Her (Jonze, 2013)

Phase VIII, Week 47–48

Turkish cinema and theory

Apply the theories you’ve learned to the cinema you know best. Turkey’s own theoretical debates (the national cinema quarrel) are also the subject of these two weeks.

  • Week 47The national cinema debate and YeşilçamSusuz Yaz (Erksan, 1963); Gurbet Kuşları (Refiğ, 1964); Vesikalı Yarim (Akad, 1968); Umut (Güney, 1970); Selvi Boylum Al Yazmalım (Yılmaz, 1977)
  • Week 48New Turkish cinemaYol (Gören & Güney, 1982); Eşkıya (Turgul, 1996); Kasaba (Ceylan, 1997); Masumiyet (Demirkubuz, 1997); Kış Uykusu (Ceylan, 2014); Mustang (Ergüven, 2015); Ahlat Ağacı (Ceylan, 2018)

Phase IX, Week 49–52

Synthesis and final

There is almost no new reading in the last four weeks; the goal is to bring what you’ve learned together into a single view and be able to defend it.

  • Week 49Redraw the map2001: A Space Odyssey (Kubrick, 1968); attractions, montage, realism and bodily experience in a single film
  • Week 50–51Capstone project
  • Week 52Final

Program

52 hafta, 9 faz. Film dilinden başlayıp klasik teoriye, auteur ve türe, göstergebilim ve psikanalize, bilişsel kurama, belgesele, dijital sinemaya ve Türkiye sinemasına uzanıyor. Her faz bir öncekinin üstüne kurulu. Dersleri yayımlanmış haftalara tıklayabilirsin.

Faz I, Hafta 1–5

Temeller

Teoriden önce dil: bir planı, bir kesmeyi, bir sesi adlandıramadan teoriyi tartışamazsın. Bu faz analiz kelime dağıarcığını kurar.

  • Hafta 1Biçim nedir?YayındaCitizen Kane (Welles, 1941)
  • Hafta 2AnlatıYayındaRashomon (Kurosawa, 1950); isteğe bağlı Memento (Nolan, 2000)
  • Hafta 3Mizansen ve sinematografiYayındaTokyo Story (Ozu, 1953) + Touch of Evil (Welles, 1958) açılış plan-sekansı
  • Hafta 4Kurgu ve sesYayındaÀ bout de souffle (Godard, 1960) + The Conversation (Coppola, 1974)
  • Hafta 5Film teorisi nedir? Haritayı çıkarYayındaSherlock Jr. (Keaton, 1924)

Faz II, Hafta 6–12

Klasik teori

Klasik teorinin ana kavgası: sinemayı sanat yapan şey gerçekliği dönüştürmesi mi (biçimciler, montaj), yoksa onu kaydetmesi mi (gerçekçiler)? Bu fazın sonunda iki tarafı da savunabilmelisin.

  • Hafta 6Sinema bir sanat mı? Münsterberg ve ArnheimYayındaSunrise (Murnau, 1927); Das Cabinet des Dr. Caligari (Wiene, 1920)
  • Hafta 7Yüz, fotojeni ve atraksiyon sinemasıLumière kısa filmleri (1895–97); Le Voyage dans la Lune (Méliès, 1902); La Passion de Jeanne d'Arc (Dreyer, 1928)
  • Hafta 8Sovyet montaj I: Kuleshov, Pudovkin, VertovChelovek s kino-apparatom / Man with a Movie Camera (Vertov, 1929); Mat (Pudovkin, 1926)
  • Hafta 9Sovyet montaj II: EisensteinBronenosets Potyomkin (1925) Odessa merdivenleri; Oktyabr (1928) “tanrılar” sekansı; modern örnek: The Godfather (1972) vaftiz sekansı
  • Hafta 10Bazin I: ontoloji ve derin odakLa Règle du jeu (Renoir, 1939); The Best Years of Our Lives (Wyler, 1946)
  • Hafta 11Bazin II ve İtalyan Yeni GerçekçiliğiLadri di biciclette (De Sica, 1948); Roma città aperta (Rossellini, 1945); Umberto D. (De Sica, 1952); bağlantı için Uzak (Ceylan, 2002) ve Close-Up (Kiarostami, 1990)
  • Hafta 12KracauerM (Lang, 1931); Nanook of the North (Flaherty, 1922)

Faz III, Hafta 13–18

Auteur, tür, yıldız

Bir filmin anlamı nereden gelir: yönetmenden mi, türden mi, yıldızdan mı, stüdyo sisteminden mi? Bu faz üç cevabı da sınar.

  • Hafta 13Auteur I: Fransa ve Cahiers du cinémaLes Quatre Cents Coups (Truffaut, 1959); Rear Window (Hitchcock, 1954); Journal d'un curé de campagne (Bresson, 1951)
  • Hafta 14Auteur II: Amerika ve yapısalcı auteurThe Searchers (Ford, 1956); Rio Bravo (Hawks, 1959); Bringing Up Baby (Hawks, 1938)
  • Hafta 15Yazarın ölümü ve auteur bir marka olarakThe Grand Budapest Hotel (Anderson, 2014); bu ay boyunca sevdiğin bir yönetmenden 3 film
  • Hafta 16Tür I: gangster ve westernScarface (Hawks, 1932); Stagecoach (Ford, 1939); Unforgiven (Eastwood, 1992)
  • Hafta 17Tür II: kuram, kara film, melodramDouble Indemnity (Wilder, 1944); All That Heaven Allows (Sirk, 1955); isteğe bağlı Chinatown (Polanski, 1974), Get Out (Peele, 2017)
  • Hafta 18Klasik Hollywood ve yıldızlarCasablanca (Curtiz, 1942); Gentlemen Prefer Blondes (Hawks, 1953)

Faz IV, Hafta 19–29

Göstergebilim, ideoloji, psikanaliz, feminizm

1960–80'lerin “büyük teori” dönemi: film bir dil gibi çözülebilir mi, seyirciyi nasıl konumlandırır, kimin bakışını dağıtır? Programın en zor ve en önemli fazı; metinler yoğun, acele etme.

  • Hafta 19Göstergebilim I: Saussure ve BarthesJulius Caesar (Mankiewicz, 1953); Ivan Grozny (Eisenstein, 1944)
  • Hafta 20Göstergebilim II: Metz ve WollenAdieu Philippine (Rozier, 1962), Metz bu filmi dizimlere ayırır; Psycho (Hitchcock, 1960)
  • Hafta 21Yapısalcı anlatı ve metinsel analizThe Birds (Hitchcock, 1963); Star Wars (Lucas, 1977)
  • Hafta 22Frankfurt Okulu ve BenjaminModern Times (Chaplin, 1936); Triumph des Willens (Riefenstahl, 1935), propaganda çözümlemesi olarak
  • Hafta 23Althusser ve Cahiers'nin ideoloji eleştirisiYoung Mr. Lincoln (Ford, 1939)
  • Hafta 24Brecht, Godard ve karşı-sinemaWeek-end (Godard, 1967); Tout va bien (Godard & Gorin, 1972); 2 ou 3 choses que je sais d'elle (Godard, 1967)
  • Hafta 25Üçüncü SinemaLa hora de los hornos (1968), ilk bölüm; Deus e o Diabo na Terra do Sol (Rocha, 1964); Memorias del subdesarrollo (Gutiérrez Alea, 1968); La battaglia di Algeri (Pontecorvo, 1966); bağlantı için Umut (Güney, 1970)
  • Hafta 26Psikanalizin temelleriVertigo (Hitchcock, 1958); Persona (Bergman, 1966)
  • Hafta 27Aygıt teorisi ve dikiş (suture)Peeping Tom (Powell, 1960); The Pervert's Guide to Cinema (Fiennes & Žižek, 2006)
  • Hafta 28Feminist film teorisi IVertigo (tekrar); Morocco (von Sternberg, 1930); Jeanne Dielman, 23 quai du Commerce, 1080 Bruxelles (Akerman, 1975)
  • Hafta 29Feminist film teorisi IIHalloween (Carpenter, 1978); Stella Dallas (Vidor, 1937); Cléo de 5 à 7 (Varda, 1962); Portrait de la jeune fille en feu (Sciamma, 2019)

Faz V, Hafta 30–36

Bilişsel kuram, Deleuze, fenomenoloji

1980'lerden sonra üç farklı yönde büyük teoriden kopuş: seyirciyi zihin olarak (bilişsel), sinemayı düşünce biçimi olarak (Deleuze), filmi beden deneyimi olarak (fenomenoloji) ele almak.

  • Hafta 30Post-Teori ve neoformalizmBanshun / Late Spring (Ozu, 1949); Pickpocket (Bresson, 1959); Playtime (Tati, 1967)
  • Hafta 31Bilişsel kuram: seyirci ve karakterMemento (tekrar); Lola rennt (Tykwer, 1998); The Talented Mr. Ripley (Minghella, 1999)
  • Hafta 32Deleuze I: hareket-imgeShichinin no samurai / Yedi Samuray (Kurosawa, 1954); La Passion de Jeanne d'Arc (tekrar)
  • Hafta 33Deleuze II: kriz ve zaman-imgeGermania anno zero (Rossellini, 1948); L'avventura (Antonioni, 1960); L'Année dernière à Marienbad (Resnais, 1961)
  • Hafta 34Deleuze III: geçmişin tabakaları ve düşünceZerkalo / Ayna (Tarkovsky, 1975); Bir Zamanlar Anadolu'da (Ceylan, 2011); Citizen Kane (tekrar)
  • Hafta 35FenomenolojiThe Piano (Campion, 1993), Sobchack'ın makalesi bu filmle açılır; Beau Travail (Denis, 1999)
  • Hafta 36Duygulanım ve dokunsal görmeIn the Mood for Love (Wong Kar-wai, 2000); Uncle Boonmee Who Can Recall His Past Lives (Weerasethakul, 2010); Leviathan (Castaing-Taylor & Paravel, 2012)

Faz VI, Hafta 37–43

Seyirci, kimlik ve belgesel

Teorinin “evrensel seyirci”si yerine gerçek seyirciler: farklı kişiler aynı filmi nasıl okur, kimler perdede yoktur, belgesel gerçeği nasıl kurar?

  • Hafta 37Kültürel çalışmalar ve alımlamaThe Rocky Horror Picture Show (Sharman, 1975); The Wizard of Oz (Fleming, 1939)
  • Hafta 38Seyirci, modernite ve hayran kültürüIntolerance (Griffith, 1916); Star Wars (tekrar, hayran kültürü gözüyle)
  • Hafta 39Irk, temsil ve muhalif bakışThe Birth of a Nation (Griffith, 1915), eleştirel olarak; Killer of Sheep (Burnett, 1978); Do the Right Thing (Lee, 1989); Daughters of the Dust (Dash, 1991)
  • Hafta 40Postkolonyal kuram ve aksanlı sinemaLa Noire de… (Sembène, 1966); Touki Bouki (Mambéty, 1973); Gegen die Wand / Duvara Karşı (Akın, 2004); Lawrence of Arabia (Lean, 1962), oryantalizm okuması için
  • Hafta 41Queer kuramRope (Hitchcock, 1948); Paris Is Burning (Livingston, 1990); Happy Together (Wong Kar-wai, 1997); Lola + Bilidikid (Ataman, 1999); Carol (Haynes, 2015)
  • Hafta 42Belgesel I: kiplerNuit et brouillard (Resnais, 1956); Chronique d'un été (Rouch & Morin, 1961); Primary (Drew, 1960); Salesman (Maysles kardeşler, 1969)
  • Hafta 43Belgesel II: hakikat, bellek, canlandırmaThe Thin Blue Line (Morris, 1988); Sans Soleil (Marker, 1983); Reassemblage (Trinh, 1982); The Act of Killing (Oppenheimer, 2012); Stories We Tell (Polley, 2012); isteğe bağlı Shoah (Lanzmann, 1985) bir bölümü

Faz VII, Hafta 44–46

Postmodernizm ve dijital sinema

Filmler artık çoğunlukla filme çekilmiyor ve salonda izlenmiyor. Bu faz, kitabın başındaki Bazin sorusuna geri döner: imgenin gerçeklikle bağı kopunca sinema nedir?

  • Hafta 44PostmodernizmBody Heat (Kasdan, 1981); Blue Velvet (Lynch, 1986); Blade Runner (Scott, 1982); Pulp Fiction (Tarantino, 1994); The Truman Show (Weir, 1998)
  • Hafta 45Dijital I: indeksin kriziJurassic Park (Spielberg, 1993); The Matrix (Wachowski kardeşler, 1999); Russkiy kovcheg / Rus Gemisi (Sokurov, 2002); Gravity (Cuarón, 2013)
  • Hafta 46Sinemadan sonra sinemaDemonlover (Assayas, 2002); Boarding Gate (Assayas, 2007); Tangerine (Baker, 2015, iPhone ile çekildi); Searching (Chaganty, 2018); Her (Jonze, 2013)

Faz VIII, Hafta 47–48

Türkiye sineması ve kuram

Öğrendiğin kuramları en iyi bildiğin sinemaya uygula. Türkiye'nin kendi kuram tartışmaları (ulusal sinema kavgası) da bu iki haftanın konusu.

  • Hafta 47Ulusal sinema tartışması ve YeşilçamSusuz Yaz (Erksan, 1963); Gurbet Kuşları (Refiğ, 1964); Vesikalı Yarim (Akad, 1968); Umut (Güney, 1970); Selvi Boylum Al Yazmalım (Yılmaz, 1977)
  • Hafta 48Yeni Türkiye sinemasıYol (Gören & Güney, 1982); Eşkıya (Turgul, 1996); Kasaba (Ceylan, 1997); Masumiyet (Demirkubuz, 1997); Kış Uykusu (Ceylan, 2014); Mustang (Ergüven, 2015); Ahlat Ağacı (Ceylan, 2018)

Faz IX, Hafta 49–52

Sentez ve final

Son dört hafta yeni okuma yok denecek kadar az; amaç öğrendiklerini tek bir görüşte birleştirmek ve savunabilmek.

  • Hafta 49Haritayı yeniden çiz2001: A Space Odyssey (Kubrick, 1968); atraksiyon, montaj, gerçekçilik ve bedensel deneyim tek filmde
  • Hafta 50–51Bitirme projesi
  • Hafta 52Final
